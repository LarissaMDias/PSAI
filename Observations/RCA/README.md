# Regional Cabled Array (RCA) Data

The [Regional Cabled Array (RCA)](https://oceanobservatories.org/regional-cabled-array/), a subset of the Ocean Observatories Initiative (OOI), is an autonomous set of more than 140 instruments that collect measurements off of the Oregon and Washington shelves. The RCA is powered by electro-optical cables, which also allow real-time transmission of data from sensor to shore station. More information on the RCA can be found on its' website.

RCA data used span from 04-17-2014 through present, and include information on salinity, temperature, oxygen, chlorophyll a, NO3, NH4, TA, and DIC.

The incorporation of RCA data into PSAI was allowed by additional funding through the University of Washington College of the Environment's Collaborative Research in Earth System Science and Technology (CRESST) funding and collaboration with the RCA team, including Debbie Kelley, Joe Duprey, Mariela Rose T. White, Michael F. Vardaro, and Wendi Ruef.
